# Recruitment Root Cause Analysis

## Objective

Identify and validate the process, data, organizational and operational factors associated with recruitment performance gaps and early employee attrition.

## Analysis Scope

The analysis went beyond the recruitment process itself and considered the wider system:

- recruitment demand
- candidate-flow data
- sourcing channels
- acquisition / communication
- process execution
- recruitment capability
- training needs
- evaluation and follow-up
- onboarding
- governance and decision-making

## Cause Categories

### People & Capability

- recruitment capability gaps
- role or métier knowledge gaps
- interviewer consistency
- candidate assessment practices
- communication practices
- coaching needs
- onboarding ownership

### Process

- unclear workflow
- excessive handoffs
- inconsistent screening
- missing controls
- rework
- weak recruitment–operations interfaces

### Data & Tools

- fragmented candidate information
- inconsistent definitions
- limited visibility
- missing historical tracking
- insufficient flow analysis
- opportunities for automation
- manual reporting effort

### Sourcing & Acquisition

- channel mix
- channel quality
- insufficient sourcing diversification
- communication / acquisition alignment
- variation in candidate quality by source

### Governance

- unclear accountability
- weak review routines
- delayed escalation
- insufficient Recruitment–Operations alignment
- decisions not consistently linked to evidence

### Candidate Experience

- expectation mismatch
- communication gaps
- process delays
- insufficient role transparency
- weak follow-up

## Validation Logic

**Problem → Data Collection → Hypothesis → Evidence → Validation → Prioritization → Action**

The principle was to first organize and understand the available information, then determine which causes required action.

A plausible cause is not automatically a confirmed root cause.

## Analysis Methods

Depending on the question and data available:

- Process analysis
- Funnel / flow analysis
- Pareto
- 5 Whys
- Cause & Effect
- segmentation
- channel comparison
- Chi-Square
- Logistic Regression
- KPI / trend analysis

## From Analysis to Action

Findings were translated into coordinated actions across:

**Process + Data + Channels + Tools + Capability + Training + Coaching + Onboarding + Governance**

The objective was not simply to identify problems, but to establish a structured sequence from evidence to decision and implementation.

## Confidentiality

Candidate-level and employee-level data are not published in this portfolio repository.
