# Recruitment Data, Sourcing & Acquisition Framework

## Purpose

Recruitment performance requires visibility across both the **candidate flow** and the mechanisms generating that flow.

The transformation therefore connected data collection, funnel analysis, sourcing channels, communication and acquisition actions.

## Data Flow

**Demand → Source → Candidate Entry → Screening → Interview → Selection → Training / Onboarding → Early Retention**

At each stage, the objective is to capture enough information to understand:

- volume
- conversion
- losses
- lead time
- quality
- source contribution
- outcome

## Sourcing & Channel Analysis

Channels should be evaluated using more than raw candidate volume.

Useful dimensions include:

- volume generated
- progression through the funnel
- quality of profiles
- conversion
- time to fill
- onboarding completion
- downstream retention

This helps distinguish a high-volume channel from a channel producing more relevant candidates.

## Communication & Marketing Alignment

Recruitment acquisition may require coordination with communication or marketing activities.

The management model can connect:

**Recruitment Need → Acquisition Objective → Channel / Campaign → Candidate Flow → Funnel Performance → Decision**

The purpose is to make acquisition actions measurable and connected to workforce requirements.

## Automation Opportunities

Once the flow and data are understood, automation opportunities can be identified in areas such as:

- data consolidation
- candidate tracking
- reporting
- alerts
- status updates
- repetitive administrative tasks
- dashboard refresh
- workflow handoffs

Automation should follow process clarification and data standardization rather than automate an unclear process.

## Management Principle

**Collect → Structure → Analyze → Identify Opportunity → Automate Where Valuable → Monitor**

The objective is to reduce manual effort while improving visibility and decision quality.

## Confidentiality

No candidate-level data, campaign details, proprietary tools or internal configurations are published.
