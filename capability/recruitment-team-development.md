# Recruitment Team Development

## Purpose

Recruitment performance depends not only on process design, but also on the capability of the people operating the process.

The transformation therefore included a structured development approach for recruitment roles.

## Roles Covered

Development was adapted to different responsibilities, including:

- Recruitment Officer / Recruitment Specialist
- Recruitment Manager / Recruitment Lead

## Capability Areas

### 1. Role & Métier Knowledge

Ensure recruiters understand:

- the roles being recruited
- operational requirements
- candidate expectations
- key selection criteria
- workforce constraints and priorities

### 2. Recruitment Techniques

Development can cover:

- sourcing
- screening
- interviewing
- candidate qualification
- structured evaluation
- follow-up
- communication

### 3. Assessment & Decision Quality

The objective is to improve consistency in:

- applying selection criteria
- evaluating candidate profiles
- documenting observations
- making evidence-based recommendations
- identifying mismatches early

### 4. Communication

Recruitment communication was treated as an operational capability:

- candidate communication
- internal communication
- expectation setting
- feedback
- coordination with Operations / Training / HR

## Learning-to-Performance Cycle

Training was not considered complete when the session ended.

The operating cycle was:

**Training Need → Training → Application → Observation → Evaluation → Feedback → Coaching → Follow-up**

This allowed training needs to be revisited based on observed performance.

## Coaching

Coaching was used to reinforce application and address specific gaps identified through:

- reporting
- observation
- quality of recruitment decisions
- candidate-flow results
- stakeholder feedback
- KPI trends

## Management Principle

**Train for the required capability → Observe application → Evaluate → Coach → Reassess**

This connects people development directly to operational performance.

## Confidentiality

No individual employee assessment, training record or personal data is included in this public case study.
