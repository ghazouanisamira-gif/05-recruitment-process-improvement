# Recruitment KPI Framework

## KPI Architecture

Recruitment KPIs should cover the complete operating chain rather than only recruitment volume.

| Category | KPI | Management Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Recruitment Demand | Understand workforce requirement |
| Planning | Planned vs. Delivered | Monitor execution against need |
| Acquisition | Source Volume | Understand channel contribution |
| Funnel | Stage Conversion | Diagnose candidate flow |
| Speed | Recruitment Lead Time | Identify bottlenecks |
| Quality | Candidate / Selection Quality | Monitor hiring effectiveness |
| Readiness | Training / Onboarding Completion | Monitor transition into operations |
| Capability | Training Effectiveness | Verify development impact |
| Outcome | Early Attrition | Assess early retention |
| Service | SLA Compliance | Monitor commitment delivery |

## KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Frequency | Owner | Action Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment Demand | Confirmed operational recruitment requirement | Weekly | Recruitment / Operations | Gap vs. capacity |
| Planned vs. Delivered | Recruitment output against approved need and plan | Weekly | Recruitment | Delivery gap |
| Source Contribution | Candidate flow generated by each sourcing channel | Weekly | Recruitment | Channel underperformance |
| Stage Conversion | Candidates progressing between defined stages | Weekly | Recruitment | Material drop |
| Lead Time | Time from approved need to selected candidate | Weekly | Recruitment | Threshold exceeded |
| Onboarding Completion | Required onboarding activities completed | Monthly | HR / Training | Completion gap |
| Training Effectiveness | Evidence of capability application after training | Monthly | Recruitment / Management | Performance gap |
| Early Attrition | New employees leaving during defined early period | Monthly | Recruitment / HR | Threshold exceeded |
| SLA Compliance | Recruitment delivery against agreed commitment | Weekly / Monthly | Recruitment | SLA gap |

## Capability Metrics

Where recruitment capability is part of the transformation, measurement should extend beyond attendance.

A practical learning-performance loop is:

**Need Identified → Training Delivered → Application Observed → Evaluation → Coaching → Follow-up → Reassessment**

Possible indicators include:

- training completion
- knowledge / assessment result
- application quality
- evaluation consistency
- coaching completion
- improvement after follow-up

## KPI Governance Rules

Every KPI requires:

- definition
- calculation method
- data source
- owner
- frequency
- target / reference level
- inclusion / exclusion rules
- management response

## Management Principle

**Measure → Interpret → Decide → Act → Re-measure**

A KPI has value only when it supports a decision, coaching action or improvement action.
